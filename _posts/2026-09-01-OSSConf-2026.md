For the past three years, [Pavel Stříž][1] had been nudging me to present my
work on free and open-source software at [the _OSSConf_ conference][2] in
Žilina, Slovakia.

<!-- more -->

This year, I finally gave in and decided to present three technologies I have
been working with for the past several years – LaTeX3, LuaTeX, and
Markdown – and compare their approaches, strengths, and trade-offs; see
also my [presentation slides][3].

The conference had [a rich program][4], with three to four parallel sessions
running throughout each of its three days. The topics included open data, open
AI, open hardware, open-source software in education, open GIS, TeX, R and
related tools, citizen science, and much more. At any given time, there was
always at least one interesting talk to attend – and often several.

If you live in Czechia or Slovakia and are interested in these topics,
[_OSSConf 2027_][2], which will take place from June 30 to July 2, 2027, at the
same location, is well worth keeping an eye on.
