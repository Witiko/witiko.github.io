This summer, I managed to destroy my _Samsung Galaxy S22 Ultra_ in water. Its
water resistance had previously been compromised by an unauthorized
service-center lens replacement, so what should have been an otherwise
regular encounter with water turned out to be fatal.

Since then, I have been looking for a replacement while temporarily falling
back to my aging _Galaxy Note9_, which is running Android 10.

<!-- more -->

  {% include image.html url="img-2364.jpg"

I have been using Samsung phones for a long time, and this time I am
seriously considering making my next phone a foldable. I am hoping that the
increasingly popular passport-style format might let me claw back some of the
dimensions phones have gained over the years; in recent years, a phone with a
screen smaller than 6 inches has seemed almost impossible to find.

  {% include image.html url="20260916_135213.jpg"
     description="Me holding a Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra that displays this article" %}

The newly released _iPhone Duo_ makes that decision more interesting. As a
long-time Samsung user, I am watching closely to see whether Samsung can
copy some of its better ideas for the _Galaxy Z Fold9_: a proper laptop mode,
speakers that provide stereo sound both folded and unfolded, S Pen support,
MagSafe, and, given my track record of breaking phones, IP68 water and dust
resistance: an upgrade over the IP48 rating that recent _Galaxy Z Flip_ and
_Fold_ phones have been known for, even though Google has offered full IP68
dust and water resistance on its _Pixel Pro Fold_ line since the _Pixel 10 Pro
Fold_ in 2025.

  {% include image.html url="ios__q3r50hxfi1ei_large_2x.jpg"
     description="Apple iPhone Duo"
     attribution="apple.com" %}

On top of that, I would love to see a telephoto lens like the one on the _Z
Fold8 Ultra_. Despite enjoying taking photos with my DSLR, having a capable
camera with me everywhere I go seems like a necessity these days, especially
when I am with my kid.

Give me all of that, and I would be more than happy to make the _Z Fold9_ my
first-ever _Fold_.
